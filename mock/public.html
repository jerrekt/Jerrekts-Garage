Questions

Who can see my car?

You and Jerrekt. Nothing about your car shows on this public page unless you turn that on later.

Why sign in with Discord?

The garage runs on a Discord bot, so you'll log work with slash commands anyway. Signing in with the same account means no passwords and no spam sign-ups.

What do I get?

A due list by miles and months, a history of every job, and parts kits with FCP Euro links for BMWs. Log from your phone with /garage log.

Can I delete it?

Yes. /garage remove deletes your car and its log. Jerrekt can't read a deleted log either.
