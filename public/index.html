The bays Public

Loading the garage…

Every job is logged with date and mileage from Discord; the garage works out what's due by miles or months, whichever comes first. Only cars switched to public show here. VINs and plates never do.

Add your car

  1. Ask Jerrekt on Discord. Tell him the car. He adds you to the garage list, which is the spam filter: no sign-ups, no passwords.
  2. Add it yourself from your phone: /garage addcar name:2008 Civic Si chassis:civic miles:112000
  3. Jerrekt approves it. Every car is approved by hand. Until then it's just a request.
  4. Log as you go: /garage log car:civic job:Oil change miles:112300 and /garage due car:civic tells you what's next.

Questions

Who can see my car?

You and Jerrekt. Nothing about your car shows on this page unless you turn that on later, and even then: no VIN, no plate, no name.

Why Discord?

The garage runs as a Discord bot, so you log work with slash commands anyway. Same account, nothing new to sign up for.

What do I get?

A due list by miles and months, a history of every job, and for BMWs parts kits with FCP Euro links (lifetime warranty, so you want to know what you bought). /garage parts job:valve-cover.

Can I delete it?

Yes. /garage remove car:civic deletes the car and its whole log. Jerrekt can't read a deleted log either.
