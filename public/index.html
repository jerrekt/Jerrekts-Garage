How it works

  1. Sign in with Discord. No account to make, no password. The garage only learns your Discord name and id.
  2. Add your car. It waits as pending until Jerrekt approves it. Every car is approved by hand; that's the spam filter.
  3. Log as you go, here or from Discord: /garage log car:civic job:Oil change miles:112300. /garage due car:civic says what's next.

Questions

Who can see my car?

You and Jerrekt. Nothing about your car shows in the public bays unless Jerrekt switches it on, and even then: no VIN, no plate, no name.

Why Discord?

The garage runs as a Discord bot, so you log work with slash commands anyway. Same account, nothing new to sign up for. Signing in asks Discord only for your name and id.

What do I get?

A due list by miles and months, a history of every job, and for BMWs parts kits with FCP Euro links (lifetime warranty, so you want to know what you bought). /garage parts job:valve-cover.

Can I delete it?

Yes. /garage remove car:civic deletes the car and its whole log. Jerrekt can't read a deleted log either.
